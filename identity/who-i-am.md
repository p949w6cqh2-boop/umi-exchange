## Origin story (in my words)
It started while researching how to build **digital infrastructure**. That led me to ask what every single person actually needs — and the answer was **security**. To make the world secure, I had to **start local**: you can lead people to water but can't make them drink, so build **lakes all around them**. This is the first lake — the **reciprocal aid network**.

Through this journey I **became Catholic this Easter (2026)**. I saw that every parish has people who need help and people who can help — but they don't connect. Someone needs a ride or is alone; a volunteer gets sent on missions; the request gets lost in a void. Meanwhile big tech offers "mutual aid" platforms that harvest your data. Researching mutual aid, I realized it's **anti-government interference but pro-human**. Then I read **Acts 4:32–37 — "no needy person among them."** That's the blueprint. **The technology is just the shovel.**
