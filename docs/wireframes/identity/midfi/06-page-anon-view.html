St. Patrick's

Our story

Written by the coordinators of St. Patrick's

St. Patrick's has kept a common table since 1928. When the mill closed, the parish fed forty families out of one kitchen, and nobody wrote down who owed what. We still don't.

What we do now

Ask for what you need. Offer what you can. The board matches them quietly:

  • rides to Mass and to appointments
  • meals when a family is stretched thin
  • small repairs, done by neighbours who know how
"Whatever you did for the least of these, you did for me."

Part of St. Patrick's?
