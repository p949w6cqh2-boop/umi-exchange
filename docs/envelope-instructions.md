# The sealed envelope: instructions for its two keepers

> Print this page and seal it inside the envelope. The design behind it is
> `docs/key-custody-design.md` (§The design, item 3, and the Fifth entry); the roles are in
> `docs/governance.md`. No names belong on this page in the repository; write them on the printed copy.

## What this envelope is

The board keeps some private information locked, and the steward keeps the keys. This envelope is
the spare. It lets the parish get back in if the steward cannot: illness, an accident, or leaving.

## What is inside (the steward fills this in before sealing)

- [ ] This page.
- [ ] **The identity sheet:** one line of text starting `AGE-SECRET-KEY-1`. It unlocks the key file.
- [ ] **The key file** (`keys.env.age`), on a USB drive and printed as text. It is replaced after
      every key change, because the identity alone unlocks nothing.
- [ ] The succession note from `docs/governance.md` (§Succession and removal).
- Sealed on ____________ by ____________ and ____________ (both sign across the seal).

## Who may open it, and when

- **Two people together: the pastor and the named trustee. Never one person alone.**
- Open it only when the steward cannot be reached and the board has to be stopped, handed over or
  rebuilt, or when the pastor names a new steward.
- **A demand for information is never a reason to open it.** A subpoena, a government agency, or
  anyone else asking for data goes to the steward and the pastor together, and to a lawyer, before
  anything moves (`docs/incident-response.md`).

## How to open it

1. Both keepers are present. Write the date, the time, both names and the reason on the line at the
   bottom of this page.
2. Give what is inside only to the person the pastor names to run the board.
3. Within the week, that person changes the keys and the identity, and a new envelope is sealed.

## Never

- Photograph it, copy it, or type it into a phone, an email or a chat.
- Take it out of the safe except to open it as above.
- Hand it to anyone because they ask, whoever they say they are.

## If it is lost, damaged, or found opened

Tell the steward the same day. The keys get changed and a new envelope is sealed. Nobody is in
trouble for reporting it; the only mistake is staying quiet.
