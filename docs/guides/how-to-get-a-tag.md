# How to get a tag

*A two-minute guide for members. Tags are optional — you can ask for help and
offer help without ever touching them.*

## What a tag is (and why it exists)

A **tag** is a small label on your profile that tells your neighbours what you
can do or who you are: *Driver*, *Cook*, *Nurse*, *SVdP Member*, *Clergy*.

Tags come in two strengths, and the difference is the whole point:

- **Self-reported** (grey) — you said it about yourself. Neighbours see it, but
  the site never dresses it up as anything more.
- **Verified** (green, with a check mark) — a coordinator confirmed it. This is
  the one that matters for trust: roles like *Clergy* or *Nurse* can only wear
  the green badge after a real person checked.

That split protects the vulnerable: nobody can style themselves "Father Jim,
verified" just by typing it. A false claim of authority never looks endorsed.

## The three steps

1. **Claim it.** Open **My Tags** from the menu (it lives at
   `/c/<your-community>/tags/`), pick the tag from your community's list, and
   claim it. It appears immediately — as *Self-reported*, grey.

2. **Ask to be verified** *(optional)*. On the same page, press **Request
   verification** on your tag and say a word about why ("I drive for the food
   bank on Saturdays"). Your request joins the coordinators' queue. You can
   make a handful of these requests per hour — enough for anyone acting in
   good faith.

3. **A coordinator decides.** Someone your community already trusts reviews
   the request — in person, after Mass, over the phone, however your community
   does trust — and approves or declines it. Approved: your badge turns
   **green Verified**. Declined: it shows *Not approved*, and you can ask
   again later after talking it over.

## What the badges mean at a glance

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Verified** | A coordinator confirmed this. |
| 🟡 **Pending** | Waiting in the coordinators' queue. |
| 🔴 **Not approved** | A coordinator declined the request. |
| ⚪ **Self-reported** | The member said it; nobody has checked. |

Only **verified** tags ever appear next to your name on the community board —
self-reported ones stay on your own profile, so the feed never implies an
endorsement that didn't happen.

## For coordinators

Verification requests arrive in your queue at `/c/<your-community>/tags/…` →
**Verification queue**. Approve what you can vouch for, decline what you
can't, and revoke a verified tag if it stops being true. Every decision is
recorded in the community's audit log.
