# The Why — in Jasiah's words

> STATUS: Jasiah's testimony, 2026-06 (lightly cleaned from a voice note; **verbatim intent
> preserved** — Jasiah edits freely). This is the soul of UMI; every other node serves it.

Everyone is lost. The soul is lost — Christian, atheist, president, peasant, homeless, poor,
plumber — everyone is searching for security: for something **unchanging**.

People aren't unchanging. But they still need community — even the people already inside one.
So you build something they can instantly go to whenever they feel lost. That is **God**.

The whole of the Law is two things: **love God with all your heart, mind, and soul — and love
your neighbour as yourself.** Give God all your time, all your love (which is your actions), all
your thoughts, all your faith and hope. Do that, and loving your neighbour comes easily — because
**when you give to the poor, you give to God.** Charity is love; loving others *for the glory of
His name* is how the two commandments become one act.

So the tangible way to do this is to **give to your neighbour** — build community, show how much
you love other people. The clothes off your back, the food from your mouth, the money from your
pocket, the love from your heart. And if they want to give back, **let them** — iron sharpens
iron. We grab each other's hands and we learn from each other; we build security in one another.
When someone pours their soul out for the Lord's glory alone, you are building something
everlasting — a monument; you are pouring your soul into the Eucharist, indulging in the love of
God.

God gave you that person. God gave you your life, your body, your skills, your love — love, out
of love. Imagine how deep His love is. So giving love to someone else is you digging deeper into
an **infinite love**. If you feel lost, dig deeper into it. If you need to be found, dig deeper —
He is there, running toward you, arms open. He's already met you 99.9 to your 0.1; He just wants
that last step, and He'll cross the rest of the way with open arms. You are seen. You are loved.

**UMI is a simple, almost-effortless network built so you can indulge in your neighbour — with
love, with charity, with faith, with strength, and with a pure understanding.** The poor are not
a market. They are neighbours.

> Blueprint: **Acts 4:32–37.** "Consent, not surveillance. The water is everywhere; nobody owns the pipes."
