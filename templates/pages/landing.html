How it works
01

Put it on the board

Post an ask or an offer. It can be groceries, a lift, or an hour of your Tuesday. A line or two is plenty.

02

A person matches it

A coordinator you know pairs asks with offers. No algorithm decides who deserves help.

03

Say yes, then talk

Contact details are shared only after both sides say yes, and only between the two of you.

Dignity is a
design decision.

{% include "illustrations/_carrying.html" with class="w-full h-auto" %}

Matched by people who know the place

Coordinators are named, accountable members of the community. Every match and every disclosure leaves a permanent record.

Private until both sides agree

Names and phone numbers never sit on a public list. Sensitive records are encrypted so thoroughly the software itself can forget them.

Yours to run, yours to link

An open protocol any parish can self-host. Communities link to each other directly, with no platform in the middle to answer to.
